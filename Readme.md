# Amazon Sales Data Analysis

**Project ID:** 78G0OL
**Internship:** Data Analyst Intern — InnoByte Services
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon sales transaction data to identify sales trends, product preferences, fulfilment performance, customer behavior, and geographical sales patterns.

The analysis was developed using Microsoft Power BI and presented through an interactive dashboard with supporting business insights and recommendations.

## Problem Statement

Analyze and provide meaningful insights from the Amazon Sales Report to support business decision-making.

## Key Objectives

* Analyze overall sales trends and patterns over time.
* Identify popular product categories, sizes, and quantities.
* Evaluate the effectiveness of different fulfilment methods.
* Analyze customer segmentation based on available customer behavior and location data.
* Study sales distribution across geographical regions.
* Provide actionable business recommendations for improving sales, inventory, and customer service.

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

* High-performing product categories and sizes
* Monthly sales patterns
* Sales contribution from different fulfilment methods
* Geographical areas with stronger sales performance
* Order and courier status patterns
* B2B and Non-B2B customer participation

Exact numerical findings are presented in the accompanying project report and Power BI dashboard.

## Business Recommendations

* Prioritize inventory for consistently high-performing categories and sizes.
* Use geographical sales performance to support regional marketing and inventory planning.
* Monitor cancelled and unshipped orders to identify operational improvement opportunities.
* Regularly compare fulfilment performance to maintain service quality.
* Use monthly sales trends for demand forecasting and campaign planning.
* Explore targeted B2B strategies based on customer participation.
